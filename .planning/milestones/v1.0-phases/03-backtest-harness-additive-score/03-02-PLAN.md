**As an** NFL fan, **I want to** see a feel-good score that is an additive, backtested composite and watch every team's mood over time, **so that** "you should feel better than you do" rests on evidence, not vibes.

Build the additive score engine and earn its first two non-Elo factors by backtest: z-score per snapshot, Σ weight × z with stored contributions, walk-forward ridge composites in `fgs backtest`, a weight proposal fit only on 1999–2021, the committed weights.yaml, and the factor delta log.

Purpose: MODEL-14 (additive engine), MODEL-03/MODEL-04 (each factor's measured backtest delta and sign), BACK-02 (composites compared against the baselines). Wiring into the site happens in plan 03-03.
Output: score.py, ridge in backtest.py, `--propose-weights`, weights.yaml, BACKTEST_LOG.md, tests.
</objective>
