# Phase 8 Plan 03: Streaks, records and RECORDS_CHECK Summary

Every snapshot now has a calendar date. Week 0 is the last day of that year's draft, and week N is the last played gameday of that week. Misery runs (score under 20) and joy runs (80 or higher) are counted in calendar days across season boundaries. The pipeline also produces weekly streak callouts ("Titans fans have been miserable for 756 straight days"), a `records.json` covering team-season extremes and longest streaks, and `pipeline/RECORDS_CHECK.md`, which is rewritten on every export or refresh.

## What was built

- **season_config**: `DRAFT_END` (last day of each draft, 1999–2026), `draft_date(season, as_of)` (falls back to Sept 1, never later than `as_of`), `MISERY_MAX=19`, `JOY_MIN=80`, `STREAK_CALLOUT_MIN_DAYS=100`.
- **insights**: `snapshot_dates`, `timelines` (drops the seeded week-0 rows of CLE 1999 and HOU 2002, and raises if any snapshot has no date), `runs`, `all_runs`, `streak_callouts` and `date_text`. `callouts_by_week(..., games=, as_of=)` orders callouts as gaps, then misery, joy, mover_up and mover_down.
- **checks**: `check_draft_dates` fails `fgs check` for any past season missing from the table; `warn_draft_date_current` only warns for the current season. Both are registered as `week0_dates`.
- **records**: `team_season_extremes`, `final_records`, `streak_records`, `active_streaks`, `build_records` and `records_check_md`. All are pure and stay inside the firewall: `records.py` was added to both leakage-test lists.
- **export/cli**: `as_of = generated_at[:10]`, `files["records.json"]`, and `_write_records_check`, which writes to `paths.RECORDS_CHECK_PATH` after `write_export` (inside the refresh marker bracket).

## RECORDS_CHECK review

I compared each list against known NFL history. **No data bugs found.** No misery run contains a winning season, and no joy run contains a losing one.

- **Matches history:** 2007 NE 16-0 is #1 happiest. 2017 CLE 0-16 is #2 saddest, and 2016 CLE 1-15 is #6. The 2010s Patriots appear three times on the joy list (2010–14, 2016–19, and 2005–09 including the 16-0 year). The Peyton-era Colts (2004–08), the Legion of Boom Seahawks (2012–16) and Mahomes-era KC (two runs) are all there. The Jaguars' 2012–15 collapse is the longest misery run. The current TEN/NYJ misery and BUF joy are active, as the research expected.
- **2008 DET 0-16 ranks #14, not top 10.** This is expected under the locked min-score rule. The Lions entered 2008 at Elo ~1440, so their lowest score was 1, not 0. The notable non-entries section shows the rank. Their 2008–10 run is #5 on the misery list.
- **2013 JAX (4-12) ranks above 2017 CLE (0-16).** Both hit 0. The tiebreak is the unrounded REG mean, and both round to 1. 2013 JAX started 0-8 with an extreme point differential, so this is defensible, but the verifier should know that #1 is decided by the tiebreak.
- **The 2005–09 Raiders run is split (model behavior, not a bug).** The LV run covers 2005 wk 16 to 2008 postseason (1,217 d). The 2009 week-0 regression toward the mean (score 26) and weeks 1–2 (22, 25) break it before misery resumes in week 3. The well-known 2003–09 stretch therefore appears as more than one run.
- **CAR 2023–25 includes an 8-9 season (2025).** Carolina scored 6–18 every week of 2025 while finishing 8-9. That is a losing record, so it is not a contradiction. The score reads the efficiency and point-differential profile as weak, which conflicts with the "won the division" story. The verifier should note this but it is not a bug.
- **CLE 2015–18 ends in 2018 wk 16 at 7-8-1,** after the late-2018 Mayfield surge. ARI 1999–2001 ends at 7-9. Both are losing records, so they are consistent.
- **Expansion seeds:** CLE 1999 and HOU 2002 are excluded from the extremes. HOU 2002–04 (770 d, #10) counts only from 2002 week 1, so it reflects results.
- **Top-10 boundaries are fragile (Pitfall 9).** The saddest positions 7–10 differ from the research prototype (2009 DET, 2000 CLE, 2002 CIN and 2008 LA were in it then). All of them are minimum-0 seasons separated by REG mean.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Check registry name collided with the draft-cache check filter**
- **Found during:** Task 3 (full suite)
- **Issue:** `test_draft.py::test_checks_draft_coverage_and_sum` runs every `CHECKS` entry whose name starts with `draft` against `{"draft": None}`. A `draft_dates` entry needs `d["games"]`, so it raised KeyError.
- **Fix:** The CHECKS and WARNINGS entries are now named `week0_dates`. The function names (`check_draft_dates`) are unchanged.
- **Files modified:** pipeline/src/fgs/checks.py, pipeline/tests/test_checks.py
- **Commit:** fce77a6

**2. Minor signature choices**
- `streak_callouts(run_df, dates, nicks, bands)` takes the precomputed `all_runs` frame instead of timelines, so `callouts_by_week` scans only once.
- `team_season_extremes` returns the full ranked lists so `build_records` can report the full-list rank of notable seasons.
