## Phase Goal

**As a** fan of an NFL team, **I want to** see when my team's record is lying, how long our misery or joy has lasted, the saddest and happiest fanbases ever and this week's biggest swings, all centered on my own team, **so that** I get shareable, evidence-backed "aha" moments about how I should really feel.

<objective>
Slice 2: make callouts and movers visible and shareable. Render the callout card strip and the biggest-movers strip on the board (latest and scrubbed weeks), the team's own callouts on its team page, and a Copy-link button with a robust fallback (SITE-05, SITE-09).

Purpose: the screenshot moment ("8-2, but you should feel worried") becomes something a user sees and shares.
Output: callouts/copyLink libs with vitest, three components, App + TeamPage wiring.
</objective>
