## Phase Goal

**As a** fan of an NFL team, **I want to** see when my team's record is lying, how long our misery or joy has lasted, the saddest and happiest fanbases ever and this week's biggest swings, all centered on my own team, **so that** I get shareable, evidence-backed "aha" moments about how I should really feel.

<objective>
Slice 3 (pipeline side of "Jets fans have been miserable for 600 straight days" and "saddest fanbase ever"). Date every snapshot, compute calendar-day misery/joy streaks (INSIGHT-03), team-season extremes (INSIGHT-04), weekly streak callouts, `records.json`, and the hand-check file `pipeline/RECORDS_CHECK.md`.

Purpose: plan 08-04 renders the records page and the team page's active-streak line from this JSON. Runs in parallel with 08-02 (web only).
Output: insights.py streak functions, records.py, draft-date table + check, export/cli wiring, RECORDS_CHECK.md, regenerated data.
</objective>
