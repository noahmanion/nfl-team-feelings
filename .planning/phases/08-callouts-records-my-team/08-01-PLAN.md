## Phase Goal

**As a** fan of an NFL team, **I want to** see when my team's record is lying, how long our misery or joy has lasted, the saddest and happiest fanbases ever and this week's biggest swings, all centered on my own team, **so that** I get shareable, evidence-backed "aha" moments about how I should really feel.

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): Users find the shareable "aha" moments: teams whose record is lying, the longest misery, the saddest and happiest fanbases ever, and this week's biggest swings, all centered on their own team.

<objective>
Slice 1 of the phase (pipeline side of "8-2 but you should feel worried" + biggest movers). Compute perception-gap callouts (INSIGHT-02) and week-over-week movers (INSIGHT-06) in a new pure `insights.py`, freeze the base-rate evidence line with a dev-only `fgs evidence` command into a committed `pipeline/callout_evidence.json` (training seasons 1999–2021 only), and ship them in the JSON contract: a `callouts` key in every `snapshots/{season}.json` and the latest week's list in `callouts.json`.

Purpose: Plan 08-02 renders these as cards and the movers strip; plan 08-03 adds misery/joy streak callouts to the same per-week lists.
Output: insights.py, evidence.py, `fgs evidence`, callout_evidence.json, export wiring, tests, regenerated web/public/data.
</objective>
