- Sims are cached at `pipeline/data/derived/sim/{model_version}.parquet`. `fgs refresh` downloads current-season pbp and recomputes current-season sims only (by digest). Target under 2 min incremental.
- vs_exp (MODEL-08): `vs_exp = ((wins + 0.5*ties) − exp_total / reg_games(season) × gp) / max(gp, 1)`. Here gp = wins + losses + ties are the snapshot's as-of REG counts. exp_total = Vegas normalized line (2003–2020, source "vegas"), else the model's week-0 sim exp_wins (source "model"). Week 0 → 0 automatically. Every row carries `expectation_source`. No hand-entered 2021+ totals (deferred, v2).
- Pitfall 1 (flag for Phase 6): vs_exp's signal comes from Vegas, and 2021+ is always model-sourced. Log THREE numbers: Δ mixed (the standard ridge step), Δ model-only (vs_exp built with vegas=None for all rows), and the deployment condition (train on mixed rows, score test seasons with model-sourced vs_exp).
- Pitfall 2: season_record and vs_exp correlate 0.92, and alpha jumps from 0.001·n to 0.01·n. Expect large offsetting bars, and season_record's weight to roughly double. That is expected, not a bug (Pitfall 9).
